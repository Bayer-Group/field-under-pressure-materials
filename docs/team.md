## One-liner

Help farmers understand weather conditions that may favour crop disease and potential treatment windows, with clear context and
uncertainty. Decisions remain with the farmer or agronomist.

## Scope

- **First iteration:** one crop (winter wheat) and one disease (septoria), with the available data classified, served by the
  backend and shown in the right place in both QGIS and the web map.
- **Direction:** a nationwide risk heatmap and field-level explanations.
- **Out of scope:** training a new model from scratch, diagnosing disease from photos, or directing a farmer to spray.
